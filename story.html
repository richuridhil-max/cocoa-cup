The Ecological Equation

Solving Two Crises with One Elegant Solution

The Dual Ecological Crisis

16 Billion Cups Discarded & Millions of Rotten Husks

Crisis 1: The Plastic-Lined Lie. Conventional single-use paper cups are lined with a hidden 5% Polyethylene (PE) plastic coating to hold liquid. They cannot be recycled in regular paper facilities and take over 30 years to fragment into billions of toxic microplastics.

Crisis 2: Agricultural Waste Burden. Kerala cocoa farmers harvest millions of cocoa pods, but the beans account for only 25% of the pod's mass. The remaining 75%—the thick outer husk—is discarded in fields, generating methane and harboring fungal Phytophthora black pod blight.

The Circular Innovation

Upcycling Kerala Waste into Luxury Tableware

Our Breakthrough: We established a clean supply chain collecting fresh cocoa husks directly from farmers in Idukki, Wayanad, and Malabar. By applying precision micronization and high-pressure hydraulic thermo-moulding, we convert this agricultural biomass into 100% plastic-free cups.

The Result: High-performance disposable cups holding 100°C boiling drinks for hours, smelling subtly of chocolate, and degrading in backyards into nutrient-rich compost in just 90 days.

Community Empowerment

Empowering Kerala's Cocoa Farming Families

For decades, smallholder cocoa farmers across Idukki, Wayanad, and Malabar treated cocoa pod husks as an inconvenient disposal problem. In wet monsoon months, rotting husks bred hazardous crop fungal pathogens.

By establishing formal collection centers and offering guaranteed buyback rates for sun-dried husks, COCOACUP™ turns discarded farm byproduct into reliable supplementary income, cleaner farm grounds, and dignity for agricultural communities.
