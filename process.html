The 5-Stage Transformation Cycle

1
Phase 01 • Raw Preparation

Industrial Solar & Thermal Drying

Freshly harvested cocoa pod husks collected from Kerala cocoa plantations arrive with ~80% moisture. They undergo clean hot-air dehydration in industrial continuous dryers, lowering moisture content down below 8% to preserve natural cellulose tensile strength and prevent microbial decay.

< 8% Moisture
Target Moisture Content
Zero Chemical Washes
Purity Protocol
Thermal Dehydration & Fiber Stabilization
2
Phase 02 • Micronization

High-Energy Micron Milling

The dried cocoa husks are conveyed into specialized stainless steel impact pulverisers, grinding the fibrous husk into an ultra-fine, homogeneous organic powder (80–120 mesh). This micro-particulate size unlocks the natural lignin and gives the finished cup an extraordinarily smooth surface finish.

80 - 120 Mesh
Micro-Particle Fineness
High-Lignin Extract
Natural Structural Fiber
Precision Micronization & Aerodynamic Grading
3
Phase 03 • Natural Formulation

Eco-Binder & Water Blending

The refined cocoa powder is blended with purified water and proprietary 100% plant-based edible binders in heated planetary mixers. This creates an elastic, cohesive organic matrix without the addition of any microplastic polymers, bisphenols, or chemical solvents.

100% Plant-Based
Natural Eco-Binder System
Zero PFAS / Solvents
Non-Toxic Guarantee
Homogeneous Planetary Fluidization
4
Phase 04 • Thermo-Forming

Hydraulic Thermo-Compression

The blended compound is dosed into hardened tool-steel moulds mounted on high-tonnage hydraulic presses. Operating at 170°C and 180 bar pressure, the extreme heat and force induce natural thermo-polymerization of the lignin. This permanently bonds the fibers into a rock-solid, waterproof cup with a silky feel.

170°C - 190°C

5
Phase 05 • Inspection & Sterilization

Precision Rim Trimming & UV-C Hygiene

Each cup undergoes robotic perimeter trimming to guarantee an ultra-smooth rim for a luxurious sipping sensation. Cups pass through germicidal UV-C sterilization chambers and undergo continuous optical vision inspections for structural perfection before clean food-grade carton packaging.
